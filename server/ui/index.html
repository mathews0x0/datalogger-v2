WHAT YOU GET

Your riding, in full detail

The RS-Core module sits on your bike and captures full telemetry — no phone mount needed. Just ride and review.

Precision Lap Times

Know your pace to the millisecond

GPS-based start/finish detection with sector splits. Compare every lap against your personal best in real-time.

Lean Angle Tracking

See how far you're really pushing

High-frequency IMU captures your lean angles through every corner. Visualize your commitment corner by corner.

Braking & Acceleration

Find time in the transitions

Longitudinal and lateral G-force analysis. Identify where you're losing time in braking zones and corner exits.

Racing Line Overlay

Compare lines, find the fast one

Visualize your actual line on the track map. Compare lines between laps to find the fastest trajectory.

Full Lap Replay

Relive every moment with data

Replay any lap with synchronized telemetry — speed, lean, G-force, and position all animated together.

Ghost Lap™

Your theoretical best, calculated

Your ideal lap built from your best sector times. See how fast you could have gone, and chase it with live sector deltas.

HOW IT WORKS

Three steps. Zero complexity.

01

Mount the RS-Core

Compact, battery-powered module. Mounts in seconds, no wiring required. It starts recording the moment you move.

02

Ride

That's it. The RS-Core records GPS, acceleration, lean angles, and more at high frequency. Completely autonomous.

03

Review & Improve

Sessions auto-upload when you're back on WiFi. Open the dashboard and see exactly where to find time.

WHY WE BUILD THIS

Professional telemetry for every rider

Performance data shouldn't be locked behind expensive racing teams. RaceSense brings the same analysis tools used by professionals to every trackday rider. Understand your riding, measure your progress, and push your limits with confidence.
